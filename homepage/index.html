About

I work on self-evolving and continual reinforcement learning for agentic large language models — how an agent can keep producing its own training data and trustworthy rewards, learn new tasks over time, and improve itself with minimal human supervision. My two systems below tackle two complementary sides of this problem: where the training signal comes from, and how to keep old abilities while learning new ones.

Systems

Two research systems on agentic reinforcement learning.

Self-Evolving RL · Data & Reward

Self-Evolving Agentic RL

A self-evolving RL system that lets agents autonomously generate training data, obtain trustworthy diff-driven rewards, and produce next-step tasks without human annotation — extending self-evolution from policy alone to task, policy, evaluator, and infrastructure. Validated end-to-end on Qwen3.5-9B.

Continual RL · Anti-Forgetting

Agentic Continual Learning

A continual-learning system over agentic LLMs: on top of GRPO, it adds a replay buffer and policy constraints so the model keeps learning new tasks while mitigating forgetting of old abilities. Includes bucketed experience replay, distribution-aware sampling, and forgetting-aware evaluation on ClawEval.
